# OASE

Control your **OASE InScenio FM-Master Cloud** (EGC / "OASE Control") garden & pond power
controller from Home Assistant.

- 🔌 Three switchable outlets as **switches**
- 💡 The dimmable outlet as a **light** (on/off + brightness)
- 🌊 **Pump** control — on/off, power %, and flow-control **shows** (Splashy, Calm, …)
- 🌈 **RGB lighting** — per channel colour, brightness and 10 effects
- 📊 **Diagnostics** — operating hours, firmware version, connectivity, pump telemetry

Cloud-based — sign in with your OASE Control **email and password**. No local network setup,
sniffing, or static IPs required. Supports re-authentication and reconfiguration.

Requires Home Assistant 2026.3.0+ and an OASE Control account with a paired FM-Master **Cloud**
gateway.
